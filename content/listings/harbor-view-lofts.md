## The property

Harbor View Lofts is a six-storey brick conversion with 48 loft apartments, ground-floor retail and 40 parking spaces. The building was fully renovated in 2023.

## Income

- **Rent roll:** $1.12M a year at 96% occupancy.
- **Operating costs:** covered by a property manager under a five-year agreement.
- **Distributions:** paid monthly, after a 5% maintenance reserve.

## Location

Two blocks from the ferry terminal and the light rail, in one of the tightest rental markets in the country.
