## Why community solar

- **Local power.** Electricity is generated and used in the same region.
- **Shared savings.** Subscribers get credits on their bill every month.
- **Long-term contracts.** Output is sold under 20-year agreements.

## The site

The farm sits on 180 acres of former farmland, with grazing sheep keeping the grass down between panel rows.
