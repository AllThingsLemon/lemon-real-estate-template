# Editing your website

1. Go to **https://your-site.com/admin/** and click **Sign in with GitHub**.
2. Pick what to edit from the left-hand menu:
   - **Listings**: the cards on the home page and their detail pages. Drag to reorder.
   - **Categories**: the filter buttons on the home page.
   - **Pages**: About, Privacy Policy, Terms and any others.
   - **Home page**: the headline, intro image and questions section.
   - **Site settings**: logo, colors, fonts, menu and footer.
   - **Publishing**: choose how changes go live (see below).
3. Click **Save**. Your site updates in about two minutes.

## Images

Upload photos straight from your computer or phone. They're resized and
converted automatically. Square images work best for listing cards, wide ones
for banners.

## How changes go live

Under **Publishing** you can choose:

- **Publish it straight away**: every save goes live.
- **Let me preview it first**: saves become drafts with a **preview link**.
  Click **Publish** when you're happy.
- **Draft → review → publish**: drafts move through review before publishing,
  for when more than one person signs off.

After switching, wait about two minutes and reload the editor. Before switching
back to "straight away", publish or delete any unpublished drafts.

## Hiding something

Each listing and page has **Hide from the site**. Tick it to take it down
without deleting it.

## Something didn't update?

Wait a couple of minutes and refresh. If it still hasn't changed, contact your
site manager. Your live site keeps showing the last working version until it's
fixed.
